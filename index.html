Our Story

A Legacy Cooked Over Two Decades

“Every plate tells a story — of spices carried from royal kitchens, of recipes guarded across generations.”

Founded in 1998, LUMINA began as a small family kitchen and grew into one of the city's most loved fine-dining rooms. We blend time-honoured Mughlai technique with modern European craft, sourcing ingredients daily from local markets.

  • ✓ Farm-fresh produce sourced every morning
  • ✓ Recipes passed down three generations
  • ✓ Tandoor fired with oak & charcoal
  • ✓ Award-winning sommelier curation
