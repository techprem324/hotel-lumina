A little about us

Hospitality, without the fuss.

The best stays are shaped by the small things: a kind greeting, a meal worth lingering over, and a room that feels easy to return to.

LUMINA began as a family kitchen in 1998. Today, we bring that same generous spirit to every part of a visit—from a quiet night in to a table shared with friends. Our approach is simple: take care with the details, make people feel at home, and let the city unfold at its own pace.
